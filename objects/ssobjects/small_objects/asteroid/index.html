101955 Bennu, an asteroid

Astronomers have found that asteroids in different "classes" (based on color) have different sized and differently shaped orbits. This suggests that asteroids in different classes originated by different processes.

Bennus's Asteroid Type based on color

In the 1970's classification of Asteroids, Bennu is classified as a "C" type asteroid because it is dark, implying its surface is covered in primitive, carbon-rich material.. Comets in this broad category are packed with carbon compounds, primitive organics, and volatile elements dating back over 4.5 billion years to the formation of the solar system.

Spectroscopic data from missions like OSIRIS-REx have allowed more precise classifications based on both color and spectral features. In the newer (2002) Bus and Binzel classification scheme, (which is called a "taxonomy" rather than a spectral class) Bennu is classified as a B-class asteroid.

Bennus's Asteroid Type based on orbit

From its orbit, 101955 is classified as an "Apollo class" asteroid, because it has a semi-major axis \(>1\) AU and a semi-minor axis \(<1\) AU, and it therefore crosses the Earth's orbit.

Apollo class asteroids make up the largest group of near-Earth objects.
